-   :material-shield-lock:{ .lg } &nbsp; __Securing AI agents__

    ---

    Give agents autonomy without giving them your credentials.

    - **Isolation** — a separate user account or a microVM per agent.
    - **Access broker** — secrets, SSH logins and folders lent for a
      limited time, kept out of the model's path.
    - **Approval gates** — read-only production, a human for risky actions.
    - **Supply-chain controls** for what agents install.

    **Example:** [securing AI agents](projects.md#securing-ai-agents-isolation-and-access-broker).
